Zoo Map Concept

A real park map instead of a bonus panel

This is a visual direction for the next map system: one connected zoo with named districts, looping paths, a central lake, and actual exhibit spots placed along the roads. The map would start mostly empty and fill in over time as players win cards.

Entry Plaza

Ticketing, guest comforts, lighter food, and easy first impressions.

International Promenade

Global exhibits from India, China, Australia, Brazil, and beyond.

Waterfront

Aquariums, penguins, marine giants, glass-bottom rides, and cool blue habitats.

Kids Cove

Petting, performers, cute animals, snack stops, and family crowd-pleasers.

Safari

Headline savanna animals, ride-throughs, monorails, and big-draw stars.

Reptile Ridge

Snake houses, swamp creatures, indoor reptile spaces, and desert corners.

Mystery Forest

Woodland trails for primates, birds, cryptids, canines, and strange discoveries.

Expansion Grounds

A flexible space for overflow, mixed-use placements, and future growth.

Concept Notes

How this version would work

The player would start with this map mostly blank. As exhibits, restaurants, shops, and infrastructure are won, they get placed into named spots along the roads. Some spots belong strongly to one district, while connector spots sit between two districts and can support more than one theme.

Strong thematic fit
Connector or mixed fit
Flexible / overflow placement
  • Restaurants and shops can live inside the themed districts they match, instead of being separated from them.
  • International exhibits can either live in their natural animal district or cluster in International Promenade.
  • Expansion Grounds can catch overflow cards so the zoo never “runs out of room.”
  • This could later grow into a more strategic system with adjacency bonuses and path-side placements.
