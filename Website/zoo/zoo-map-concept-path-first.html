The map only carries the area names now. The fuller explanations and placement groups live here so they do not cover the map itself.

Wild Waterfront

Aquariums, penguins, glass-bottom rides, and cold-water stars.

Kids Cove

Gentle encounters, petting areas, playful performers, and family food.

Safari Plains

Reptile Ridge

Serpents, swamps, dim habitats, and intense specialist spaces.
