Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 22, 2026

Email Is Optional

You do not need to provide an email address to play. If you do provide an email address, we only use it to help you recover saved progress if you lose or switch devices, or to communicate with you about the game in rare cases.

What We Collect

CommunityVerse Games may collect the information you enter while playing Restaurant Challenge, including player name, virtual restaurant name, optional email address, trivia results, customers earned, leaderboard scores, and saved game progress.

We may also use basic technical information, such as browser or device information, to run the site and fix problems.

How We Use It

  • To save game progress and customer collections.
  • To maintain leaderboards and trivia scores.
  • To send a secure recovery link if you choose to provide an email address.
  • To respond when you contact us.
  • To improve the game using general gameplay information.

Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information. We may use trusted service providers, such as hosting, database, analytics, or email tools, only to operate CommunityVerse Games.

Leaderboards may show player names, virtual restaurant names, and game scores to other players.

Children Under 13

CommunityVerse Games is family friendly, but due to federal regulations, it is not intended for children under 13. To save progress or enter personal information, you must be 13 or older.

If you are under 13, please keep playing as a guest and do not enter your name, email address, or other personal information.

Parents or guardians can contact us to review or request deletion of information.

Review, Correction, And Deletion Requests

You may contact us to ask us to review, correct, delete, or anonymize personal information connected to a saved Restaurant Challenge profile.

To protect players, we may need to verify that the request is from the person connected to the saved profile, or from a parent or guardian if the request involves a child.

If personal information is deleted or anonymized, we may still keep non-personal game information, such as play counts, scores, customer collection progress, virtual restaurant progress, and general leaderboard records. We may remove or rename player names, email addresses, recovery information, and other details that could identify a person.

Contact Us

Use the Contact button on the Restaurant Challenge page if you have privacy questions or want to request review, correction, deletion, or anonymizing of personal information.

Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy as CommunityVerse Games changes. The effective date above shows when this page was last updated.
