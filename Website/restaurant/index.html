Restaurant Challenge

How to Play

Restaurant Challenge is a quick trivia game where each round helps your restaurant earn customers.

The Short Version

Choose a restaurant, answer 10 questions, and try to earn a customer for your own restaurant.

What Are Customers?

Customers are the people your restaurant collects when you do well in a game. They help your restaurant grow.

Occasional, Regular, and Favorite

A better score earns a stronger customer. Regular customers can become Favorites after more successful visits.

Leaderboard

The leaderboard compares restaurants by sales, customers, rating, games played, and other progress.

For Restaurant Owners

You can share your restaurant game link and let players answer local trivia while your restaurant collects customers.
